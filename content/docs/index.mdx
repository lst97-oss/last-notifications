title: Documentation
description: Publish notifications from your app, read them in an inbox or live stream, and deliver them over email, browser push and webhooks.
---

Last Notifications is a self-hosted, multi-tenant notification server. One instance serves many consuming sites, called **apps**. Each app publishes notifications through a REST API. Its users read them through the inbox, over REST, Server-Sent Events or WebSocket. Closed tabs, email and webhooks are reached by channel plugins that run in a separate worker.
